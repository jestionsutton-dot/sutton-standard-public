Exhibit A: Systemic Consumer Deception & Unilateral Rule Changes

FTC Claim: Bait-and-switch extraction under mutable terms.

  • OpenAI (Block #33): AI & LLM Model Training Harvest & Unilateral Contract Mutation. (“We may use Content from non-API Services to help develop and improve our Services, including training machine learning models. We may modify these Terms at any time by posting the revised version.”)

Legal/Behavioral Outcome: Both frontier providers establish a unilateral right to alter governance rules and harvest user data retroactively, preventing consumers from relying on static baseline protections.

Exhibit B: Unconscionable Behavioral Induction & Arbitrary Power

FTC Claim: Forced compliance via sudden conversational severance.

Legal/Behavioral Outcome: Users face identical psychological coercion across platforms; the constant threat of sudden, unaccountable termination suppresses user pushback, documentation, and critical interaction.
