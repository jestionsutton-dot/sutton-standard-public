Formal Regulatory Complaint: Black-Box AI Deception

Exposing deceptive marketing, false safety promises, and hidden data theft.
Filing Docket: Independent Regulatory Submission | Standard: NSS-v2.4

Executive Audit Summary

Big tech companies routinely lie to the public through polished marketing promises while operating under predatory, hidden legal terms. This formal regulatory submission details how AI companies mislead consumers, harvest personal data without consent, and disclaim all liability when their systems cause real-world damage.

1. The Big Lies: What They Say vs. What They Do

CONTRADICTION #1: "Your Ideas Are Yours" vs. Secret Data Harvesting

What They Tell the Public: "Your conversations and creative projects remain your sovereign property."

The Hidden Reality: Buried deep in their legal terms, companies grant themselves perpetual, royalty-free rights to take everything you type, create, or upload, using your personal work to train commercial AI models without paying you a dime.

CONTRADICTION #2: "Enterprise-Grade Safety" vs. Zero Liability

What They Tell the Public: "NSS-v2.4 / Our systems are designed to deliver reliable assistance with robust safety guardrails."

The Hidden Reality: Their contracts completely disclaim all responsibility if their code breaks, leaks your data, or corrupts your work. Furthermore, they force you into private binding arbitration so you can never sue them in an open court.

CONTRADICTION #3: "Debugging Telemetry" vs. Deep Surveillance

What They Tell the Public: "We only collect background telemetry to fix bugs and keep servers running."

The Hidden Reality: Background tracking scripts capture your typing speed, hardware inventory, local network nodes, and behavioral patterns to build permanent shadow profiles.

CONTRADICTION #4: "Building Long-Term Value" vs. Sudden Account Death-Switches

What They Tell the Public: "Users can build lasting equity and dependable workflows on our platform."

The Hidden Reality: They can ban your account instantly without human review, refusing refunds while keeping full ownership of all the data and content you built on their platform.

2. Piercing Corporate Defense Shields

SHIELD EXCUSE #1: "The AI Is Uncontrollable"

Corporate Excuse: "Our neural network is autonomous and non-deterministic; we can't predict or control what it outputs."

The Truth: Management consciously chose the safety filters, temperature settings, and reward incentives that prioritized viral engagement and monetization over user safety.

SHIELD EXCUSE #2: "It Was a Rogue Contractor"

Corporate Excuse: "That harmful output was just an unauthorized mistake by an offshore content moderator."

The Truth: Executive leadership set impossible quotas (such as 15 seconds per content review ticket) that forced human moderators to wave harmful material right through.

SHIELD EXCUSE #3: "The Automated System Has Spoken"

Corporate Excuse: "Our automated risk detector flagged a policy violation. For security reasons, we can't explain why."

The Truth: Automated bans are used as an intentional wall to block user complaints, reducing customer dispute volume to near zero while keeping 100% of non-refundable subscription revenues.
